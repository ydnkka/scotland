# Scottish SARS-CoV-2 Genomics: Selected Literature

Focused reading list for genomic transmission, outbreak investigation, travel, and socioeconomic context in Scotland. Publication status and links were checked in July 2026.

## National and outbreak genomics

- da Silva Filipe et al. (2021), [*Genomic epidemiology reveals multiple introductions of SARS-CoV-2 from mainland Europe into Scotland*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-020-00838-z), *Nature Microbiology* 6:112–122. National introductions and early phylogeography.
- Li et al. (2021), [*Genetic epidemiology of SARS-CoV-2 transmission in renal dialysis units—a high-risk community-hospital interface*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33895226/), *Journal of Infection* 83:96–103. Scottish dialysis-unit clustering and community/hospital links.
- Stirrup et al. (2021), [*Rapid feedback on hospital onset SARS-CoV-2 infections combining epidemiological and sequencing data*](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.65828), *eLife* 10:e65828. Glasgow and Sheffield hospital-onset investigation.
- Stirrup et al. (2022), [*Effectiveness of rapid SARS-CoV-2 genome sequencing in supporting infection control for hospital-onset COVID-19 infection: multicentre, prospective study*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9596156/), *eLife* 11:e78427. UK multicentre evaluation with Scottish participation.
- Nickbakhsh et al. (2022), [*Genomic epidemiology of SARS-CoV-2 in a university outbreak setting and implications for public health planning*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35853960/), *Scientific Reports* 12:11735. University of Glasgow introductions and outbreaks.
- Cotton et al. (2023), [*Investigation of hospital discharge cases and SARS-CoV-2 introduction into Lothian care homes*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhin.2023.02.010), *Journal of Hospital Infection* 135:28–36. WGS and patient-timeline assessment of care-home introductions.

## Travel, variants, and clinical epidemiology

- McLachlan et al. (2024), [*Evaluation of risk-based travel policy for the COVID-19 epidemic in Scotland: a population-based surveillance study*](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-085332), *BMJ Open* 14:e085332. National travel, importation, and genomic surveillance.
- Pascall et al. (2023), [*The SARS-CoV-2 Alpha variant was associated with increased clinical severity of COVID-19 in Scotland*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37053201/), *PLOS ONE* 18:e0284187. Scottish sequence-linked severity cohort; adjacent to transmission analysis rather than a clustering study.
- Sheikh et al. (2021), [*SARS-CoV-2 Delta VOC in Scotland: demographics, risk of hospital admission, and vaccine effectiveness*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34139198/), *The Lancet* 397:2461–2462. Variant-classified national epidemiology, including deprivation adjustment.

## Preprints on transmission and deprivation

- Lycett et al. (2021), [*Epidemic waves of COVID-19 in Scotland: a genomic perspective on the impact of the introduction and relaxation of lockdown on SARS-CoV-2*](https://doi.org/10.1101/2021.01.08.20248677), medRxiv preprint. First/second-wave introductions and lineage persistence; no peer-reviewed version was identified.
- Gamża et al. (2023), [*Spatial analysis of phylogenetic, population and deprivation data from Scottish SARS-CoV-2 outbreak reveals patterns of the community transmission*](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4655074), SSRN preprint. Dundee/Tayside spatial and deprivation analysis.
- Gamża et al. (2024), [*Infector characteristics exposed by spatial analysis of SARS-CoV-2 sequence and demographic data analysed at fine geographical scales*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10436), arXiv preprint. Later analysis from the same research programme using Tayside sequence and demographic data.

## Relevance to this project

The strongest precedents for national Scottish phylogeography are da Silva Filipe and Lycett; for bounded outbreak genomics, Li, Stirrup, Nickbakhsh, and Cotton; and for sequence-linked deprivation, the Gamża preprints. The literature does not by itself validate EpiLink clusters or transition-graph candidates as transmission chains or confirmed superspreading events.
