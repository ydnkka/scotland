| **SSE category**                               | **Short description**                                                                                      | **Typical signature**                                                                                           |
|------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `putative_birth__contained_burst`              | A newly appearing large cluster with little observed onward spread.                                        | High amplification, high novelty, low `out_strength`, low downstream expansion.                                 |
| `putative_birth__single_dominant_chain`        | A new burst that mainly continues through one successor lineage.                                           | High amplification, high novelty, low downstream entropy, high `dominant_successor_frac`.                       |
| `putative_birth__multi_branch_seeder`          | A new burst that seeds several successor clusters, but not necessarily with large total onward burden.     | High amplification, multiple outgoing edges, high downstream entropy.                                           |
| `putative_birth__multi_branch_expander`        | A new burst that seeds multiple substantial onward branches.                                               | High amplification, high `out_degree`, high `out_strength`, high downstream entropy, high downstream expansion. |
| `putative_birth__diffuse_spatial_broadcaster`  | A new burst followed by broad spatial or demographic dissemination.                                        | High amplification, high downstream entropy, high spatial breadth or high `mixing_score`.                       |
| `relay_amplifier__contained_burst`             | A cluster that inherited infections from earlier nodes, amplified locally, but did not spread much onward. | Nonzero incoming strength, high net amplification, low onward burden.                                           |
| `relay_amplifier__single_dominant_chain`       | A secondary amplification event that mainly feeds one continuing chain.                                    | Incoming links present, high amplification, one dominant outgoing successor.                                    |
| `relay_amplifier__multi_branch_seeder`         | A secondary event that splits transmission into several successor clusters.                                | Incoming links present, high amplification, high downstream entropy.                                            |
| `relay_amplifier__multi_branch_expander`       | A high-impact secondary amplifier that generates multiple substantial onward branches.                     | Incoming links present, high net amplification, high `out_strength`, high downstream expansion.                 |
| `relay_amplifier__diffuse_spatial_broadcaster` | A secondary amplifier associated with broad onward dissemination across places or groups.                  | High net amplification, high downstream entropy, high spatial breadth or mixing.                                |
| `merged_relay__multi_branch_expander`          | A node formed from multiple incoming sources that then expands into multiple onward branches.              | High `in_degree`, merging lifecycle, high `out_degree`, high `out_strength`.                                    |
| `merged_relay__single_dominant_chain`          | A merged cluster that continues mainly through one dominant successor.                                     | High `in_degree`, low downstream entropy, high `dominant_successor_frac`.                                       |
| `isolated_burst__no_observed_onward_spread`    | A large or unusual cluster with no observed incoming or outgoing network continuity.                       | Isolated node, possible sampling artefact or event outside observed window.                                     |
| `terminal_sink__no_observed_onward_spread`     | A cluster with incoming sequences but no observed onward spread.                                           | Nonzero `in_strength`, zero `out_strength`, often death/right-censored.                                         |
| `not_sse_like`                                 | Node does not meet the amplification/novelty threshold for an SSE-like event.                              | Low or ordinary amplification relative to contemporaneous clusters.                                             |
| `*_weak_or_ambiguous_onward_spread`            | Candidate SSE-like node, but onward pattern is not clearly classifiable.                                   | Some amplification signal, but weak or conflicting downstream features.                                         |


| **Family**      | **Includes**                                         | **Interpretation**                                                                |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Contained SSE   | `*__contained_burst`, `*__no_observed_onward_spread` | Large short-lived burst with limited detected onward transmission.                |
| Chain SSE       | `*__single_dominant_chain`                           | Event seeded a persistent but narrow transmission chain.                          |
| Branching SSE   | `*__multi_branch_seeder`, `*__multi_branch_expander` | Event seeded multiple descendant clusters.                                        |
| Broadcaster SSE | `*__diffuse_spatial_broadcaster`                     | Event associated with spatially/demographically broad onward dissemination.       |
| Relay SSE       | `relay_amplifier__*`, `merged_relay__*`              | Not necessarily the original introduction, but important secondary amplification. |
